sandık is a personal portfolio tracking app that lets you manage stocks, TEFAS mutual funds, forex, gold and commodities in one place.

KEY FEATURES

• Track BIST stocks, TEFAS funds (1000+ funds with live return data), forex (USD/EUR/GBP) and gold (gram, quarter, half, Ata, Reşat, Republic coins, ounce)
• Prices update automatically; manual entry also supported
• See total value, profit/loss and allocation chart in real time
• Daily portfolio performance chart based on real historical data
• Fast, natural-language bulk entry (type "10 grams of gold at 4500 lira" and it's added automatically)

TECHNICAL SIGNALS

• Directional analysis using RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, EMA crossover and Stochastic indicators
• Get notified when a strong signal appears
• Not investment advice — for informational purposes only

PORTFOLIO SHARING

• Share your portfolio with a partner or family member via invite code
• They can only view — they can't edit your assets
• End the partnership at any time

SECURITY & PRIVACY

• Data is protected with encrypted cloud storage
• Hide your balance with one tap
• GDPR-compliant data processing; delete your account anytime

Open your sandık and start tracking.
