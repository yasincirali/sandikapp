• When adding an asset, pick the trade date with the Today, Yesterday or last trading day chips. Quick buy and sell have Today and Yesterday chips too.
• When setting an alert, the "52-week high" and "52-week low" chips fill in the target with one tap.
• On an asset page, long-press the title or the price to copy the code or the price.
• In Performance, the chart no longer redraws from scratch when you change the period; it flows into the new one.
