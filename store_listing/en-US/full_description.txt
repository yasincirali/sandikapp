sandık is a personal portfolio tracking app that lets you manage all your investments in one place.

KEY FEATURES

• Add stocks, mutual funds, forex and gold assets
• Prices update automatically; manual entry also supported
• See total value, profit/loss and allocation chart in real time
• Track your daily performance history
• Securely share your portfolio with family or business partners
• End the partnership at any time

SECURITY

• All data protected with encrypted cloud storage
• App automatically hides from screenshots and task manager
• GDPR-compliant data processing

EASE OF USE

• Fast asset-adding flow
• Balance hiding feature
• Dark theme, eye-friendly interface
• Works offline with last known data

Open your sandık and start tracking.
