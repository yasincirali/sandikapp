Did your money grow, or did it just melt?

If your portfolio gained 40% while inflation was 45%, your purchasing power went DOWN. Most trackers won't tell you that — sandik will.

sandik puts your portfolio side by side with Turkey's official CPI: your nominal return, the period's inflation and the gap between them, each shown separately. Green if you beat inflation, red if you didn't.
