sandık is a personal portfolio tracker that lets you manage all your investments from one place.

CORE FEATURES

• Add stocks, mutual funds, currencies, gold, and commodities
• Prices update automatically; manual price entry supported
• See total value, gain/loss, and allocation chart instantly
• Track historical performance with a TradingView-style professional chart — pinch zoom, crosshair, dynamic time ranges
• Share your portfolio securely with family members or business partners
• End sharing at any time

COMPETITION MODE (Optional)

• Compete in return rankings with your partners
• See where you stand among all Sandık users via anonymous global percentile
• Discover the allocation patterns of top gainers with "Top Portfolios" — identity, amounts, and TRY totals are never shared
• KVKK/GDPR-compliant, opt-in only — turn it off anytime

SECURITY & PRIVACY

• All data protected with encrypted cloud storage
• App automatically hides from screenshots and task manager
• KVKK and GDPR compliant data processing
• Competition feature operates on k-anonymity principle (min. 20 participants)
• Delete your account and all data at any time

CONVENIENCE

• Quick asset add flow (with historical purchase date support)
• Bulk asset add cart
• Hide-balance mode
• Dark theme, eye-friendly UI
• Turkish/English date and currency formatting
