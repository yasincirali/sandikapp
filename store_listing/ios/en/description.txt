Gold, dollars or stocks — which one really paid off? sandık brings them together on one screen and tells you one thing clearly: what you actually earned, and whether you beat inflation.

Most trackers subtract the purchase price from today's price and call it "profit". sandık adds commissions to your cost and dividends to your gains. The number you see is the number in your pocket.

REAL RETURN VS. INFLATION

• Your return, inflation and the gap, each shown separately
• Green if you beat inflation, red if you didn't
• Data from TurkStat / the Central Bank — never estimated
