Gold, dollars, stocks or crypto: which one really paid off? sandık brings them together on one screen and tells you one thing clearly: what you actually earned, and whether your money kept up with prices.

Most trackers subtract the purchase price from today's price and call it "profit". sandık adds commissions to your cost, counts dividends as gains and accounts for when you added money. The number you see is the number in your pocket.

REAL RETURN VS. INFLATION
• "Is your money keeping up with prices?" What last year's 100 lira is worth today, on the home screen every day
• Your portfolio next to Turkey's official CPI (TurkStat): how many points ahead or behind you are, and the months it was measured over
• Data from TurkStat and the Central Bank of Turkey

EVERYTHING IN ONE PORTFOLIO
• Turkish stocks (BIST) and mutual funds (TEFAS)
• US stocks and ETFs, priced in dollars and shown in TRY at the live rate
• Crypto: every coin with a Turkish lira pair plus the 250 most-traded coins
• Currencies, gold (gram, quarter, half, full, Republic and Ata coins), silver and commodities
• Eurobonds: accrued interest, yield to maturity and the next coupon
• Time deposits (net return after withholding tax) and private pension (BES)
• Prices update automatically; enter your own price when you prefer

EASY ENTRY
• Import from statement: choose your broker's or bank's PDF, Excel or CSV statement and sandık finds the columns
• Type THYAO, Apple, BTC or an ISIN and the app finds the asset type
• Enter backdated purchases; foreign-currency assets use the rate on the purchase date
• A stock's dividends from the last 12 months come pre-filled; you just confirm

TRUE PROFIT & LOSS
• Purchase commissions included in cost, cash dividends counted as gains
• Costs card: commissions, dividend withholding tax, fund management fees and buy-sell spreads on gold and currencies
• Realized profit from sales shown separately
